# CLASSIFICATION CATEGORIES
* `Shortened life span: infancy`: A phenotype or condition that typically results in death or a significantly reduced life expectancy during the first year of life (from birth up to 24 months of age).
* `Shortened life span: childhood/adolescence`: A phenotype or condition that typically leads to death or a markedly reduced life expectancy during childhood (ages 2–12 years) or adolescence (ages 13–18 years).
* `Shortened life span: adulthood`: A phenotype or condition that usually causes death or substantially shortens life expectancy during adulthood (after age 18 years).
* `Intellectual disability`: A phenotype or condition where there are significant limitations in intellectual functioning (such as problem-solving, reasoning, or learning) and in adaptive behaviors, with onset before the age of 18.
* `Internal physical malformations`: A congenital structural anomaly that affects the shape, formation, or location of an internal organ or tissue.
* `Impaired mobility`: Any phenotype where **gross motor locomotion** is significantly limited. This applies **exclusively** to the ability to walk, run, or move the entire body. Skeletal issues are classified here only if their **primary clinical problem** is the loss of gross motor locomotion.
* `Dysmorphic feature`: An abnormality of the external body or face’s shape, size, or proportions that is observable. This includes craniofacial anomalies, **structural dental abnormalities (e.g., Dentinogenesis Imperfecta)**, and skeletal abnormalities where the primary clinical impact is on the body's form and structure, rather than a direct loss of gross motor function.
* `Sensory Impairment – Vision`: Partial or complete loss of visual ability.
* `Sensory Impairment – Hearing`: Partial or complete loss of the ability to hear.
* `Sensory impairment: touch, others`: Impairment in the ability to perceive touch, pain, temperature, or other non-visual/non-auditory sensory functions.
* `Immunodeficiency/cancer`: Immune deficiency or malignant conditions.
* `Mental illness`: A clinically significant disturbance in cognition, emotional regulation, or behavior. This category includes neurodevelopmental disorders such as autism spectrum disorder and attention-deficit disorders (e.g., 'Short attention span').
* `Infertility`: A condition where a person is unable to conceive children naturally.
* `Default (non-severe)`: Benign variation not clinically significant.
* `NFC (Not Further Classified)`: Cannot be confidently placed.
